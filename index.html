🌟 Famous People

Discover the lives and achievements of influential people.

SCIENCE

Stephen Hawking

A physicist who explored black holes and the mysteries of the universe.

Early Life

Stephen Hawking was born in Oxford, England, in 1942. He became one of the world's best-known theoretical physicists.

Scientific Work

He studied black holes, cosmology and the origins of the universe. His work helped scientists understand how black holes might emit radiation, now called Hawking radiation.

Books and Legacy

His popular book A Brief History of Time introduced complex ideas about the universe to millions of readers. He died in 2018, but his scientific contributions continue to inspire.
WORLD HISTORY

Adolf Hitler

The dictator whose Nazi regime brought devastation to Europe.

Rise to Power

Adolf Hitler was born in Austria in 1889. He became leader of the Nazi Party and was appointed chancellor of Germany in 1933. He destroyed democratic institutions and established a dictatorship.

World War II and Nazi Crimes

Nazi Germany invaded Poland in September 1939, beginning World War II in Europe. Hitler's regime promoted racist, antisemitic ideology and carried out the Holocaust, in which six million Jewish people were murdered, alongside the persecution and mass murder of millions of other victims.

Defeat

Germany was defeated in 1945. Hitler died in Berlin that year. His dictatorship demonstrates the terrible consequences of hatred, racism, propaganda and unchecked power.
SCIENCE

Albert Einstein

The physicist who transformed our understanding of space and time.

Born in Germany in 1879, Albert Einstein developed the theory of relativity. His famous equation, E = mc², describes a relationship between mass and energy. He received the 1921 Nobel Prize in Physics for his explanation of the photoelectric effect. He became one of the most influential scientists in history.
INDIAN HISTORY

Bhagat Singh

A revolutionary remembered in India's independence movement.

Bhagat Singh was born in 1907 in Punjab. He opposed British colonial rule and became involved in revolutionary activities for Indian independence. He was executed by the British colonial government in 1931, aged 23. He remains an important figure in India's history and is remembered for his courage, political ideas and commitment to independence.
WORLD HISTORY

Napoleon Bonaparte

The French military leader who became emperor.

Napoleon rose to prominence during the French Revolution and became emperor of France in 1804. His armies reshaped Europe, while his legal reforms, including the Napoleonic Code, influenced many countries. After military defeats, he was exiled and died in 1821.
INDIAN HISTORY

A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

The scientist who became India's eleventh president.

Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was born in Rameswaram in 1931. An aerospace scientist, he contributed to India's space and missile programmes. He served as president from 2002 to 2007 and inspired many students to pursue science, education and public service. He died in 2015.

📜 World and Indian History

WORLD HISTORY

World War I

The global conflict that lasted from 1914 to 1918.

World War I began in 1914 after the assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand. Alliances, militarism, nationalism and imperial rivalries contributed to the conflict. The Central Powers fought the Allied Powers. Trench warfare caused immense suffering, and the armistice of 11 November 1918 ended the fighting. The war changed borders and helped shape later events.
WORLD HISTORY

World War II

A worldwide conflict fought from 1939 to 1945.

World War II began in Europe when Nazi Germany invaded Poland in 1939. The Allies eventually included Britain, the Soviet Union, the United States, China and other nations. The war involved battles across Europe, Africa, Asia and the Pacific. The Holocaust was the systematic murder of six million Jews by Nazi Germany and its collaborators. The war ended in 1945 after Germany's surrender in May and Japan's surrender in August.
ANCIENT HISTORY

Ancient Egypt

A civilisation known for pyramids, pharaohs and hieroglyphs.

Ancient Egyptian civilisation developed along the Nile River. The river supported farming, trade and large settlements. Egyptian rulers were called pharaohs. Egyptians built pyramids, developed hieroglyphic writing and made advances in mathematics, medicine and architecture.
INDIAN HISTORY

The Maurya Empire

One of the largest empires in ancient Indian history.

Chandragupta Maurya founded the Maurya Empire around 322 BCE. Under Emperor Ashoka, the empire expanded across much of the Indian subcontinent. After the Kalinga War, Ashoka promoted Buddhist teachings, non-violence and public welfare. The empire made important contributions to Indian political history.

🔬 Science and Space

ASTRONOMY

The Solar System

Discover our Sun, eight planets and many smaller worlds.

The Solar System formed about 4.6 billion years ago. It includes the Sun, eight planets, dwarf planets, moons, asteroids and comets. Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only world currently known to support life.
PHYSICS

Black Holes

Explore regions of space with gravity so strong that light cannot escape.

A black hole forms when matter becomes concentrated enough for gravity to prevent light from escaping beyond its event horizon. Scientists study black holes using their effects on nearby matter and light. Some form when massive stars collapse.
TECHNOLOGY

Artificial Intelligence

How computers perform tasks that usually require human intelligence.

Artificial intelligence, or AI, is a field of computing focused on systems that can recognise patterns, understand language, make predictions and solve problems. AI is used in medicine, education, research and many other areas. Responsible development includes attention to safety, privacy and fairness.

🌍 Geography

GEOGRAPHY

Our Planet Earth

Explore continents, oceans, climates and landforms.

Earth is the third planet from the Sun. Its surface contains seven commonly recognised continents and five major oceans. Mountains, rivers, deserts, forests and grasslands create a wide range of environments. Earth's atmosphere and liquid water help make life possible.
INDIA

Geography of India

From the Himalayas to the Indian Ocean.

India is a country in South Asia. Its geography includes the Himalayas, the Indo-Gangetic plains, the Thar Desert, the Deccan Plateau and long coastlines. The Ganga and Brahmaputra are among its major river systems. India also has diverse climates, languages and ecosystems.

⚽ Sports Legends

FOOTBALL

Cristiano Ronaldo

A Portuguese footballer renowned for goalscoring and athleticism.

Cristiano Ronaldo was born in Madeira, Portugal, in 1985. He became a global football star after playing for clubs including Manchester United, Real Madrid and Juventus. He has won major club trophies and the UEFA European Championship with Portugal. He is widely recognised for his dedication, speed and remarkable goalscoring record.
FOOTBALL

Lionel Messi

An Argentine footballer celebrated for his creativity and skill.

Lionel Messi was born in Rosario, Argentina, in 1987. He rose to fame at Barcelona and later played for Paris Saint-Germain and Inter Miami. Messi captained Argentina to victory at the 2022 FIFA World Cup. He is known for his close control, passing, dribbling and playmaking.
CRICKET

Sachin Tendulkar

One of the most celebrated batters in cricket history.

Sachin Tendulkar was born in Mumbai in 1973. He made his international cricket debut as a teenager and played for India for more than two decades. He was a key member of India's 2011 Cricket World Cup-winning team and is admired for his technique and achievements.
