Featured Articles

World War I (1914–1918)

World War I was a major international conflict that began in 1914 and ended in 1918. Rival alliances, militarism, imperial competition and the assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand contributed to its outbreak. The war changed borders and societies around the world.

World War II (1939–1945)

World War II began in 1939 when Nazi Germany invaded Poland. The Allies, including Britain, the Soviet Union, the United States and China, fought the Axis powers. The war included the Holocaust, in which Nazi Germany murdered six million Jews, as well as millions of other victims of Nazi persecution. The conflict ended in 1945 and transformed the world.

Ancient Civilizations

Ancient Egypt, Mesopotamia, Greece, Rome and the Indus Valley developed cities, writing, trade, government and remarkable architecture. Their achievements influenced later societies.

Indian History

Indian history includes the Indus Valley Civilization, the Maurya and Gupta empires, regional kingdoms, the Mughal period and British rule. India became independent on 15 August 1947.

The Solar System

The Solar System consists of the Sun and objects held by its gravity, including eight planets, moons, dwarf planets, asteroids and comets. Earth is the third planet from the Sun.

Earth and Its Continents

Earth has seven commonly recognized continents and five commonly recognized oceans. Its landscapes include mountains, deserts, forests, plains and rivers.

Biographies

A biography describes a person's life, experiences, achievements and influence. Learning about historical figures helps us understand how people have shaped the world.

Football

Football, also called soccer in some countries, is played by two teams of eleven players. The FIFA World Cup is the leading international tournament in men's and women's football.

Computers and Technology

Computers process information and help people communicate, learn, create and solve problems. Modern technology includes robotics, artificial intelligence and the internet.

Literature

Literature includes novels, short stories, poetry, plays and other written works. It shares ideas, explores human experiences and preserves cultures.
