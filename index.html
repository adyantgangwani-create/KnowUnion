Featured Articles

World War I (1914–1918)

World War I was a major international conflict that began in 1914 and ended in 1918. Rival alliances, militarism, imperial competition and the assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand contributed to its outbreak. The war reshaped borders and societies around the world.

World War II (1939–1945)

World War II was a global conflict that began in 1939 when Nazi Germany invaded Poland. The Allies, including Britain, the Soviet Union, the United States and China, fought the Axis powers. The war included the Holocaust, in which Nazi Germany murdered six million Jews, as well as millions of other victims of Nazi persecution. It ended in 1945 and transformed the world.

Ancient Civilizations

Ancient civilizations developed cities, systems of government, writing, trade and remarkable architecture. Ancient Egypt, Mesopotamia, Greece, Rome and the Indus Valley made lasting contributions to human history.

Indian History

The history of India includes the Indus Valley Civilization, the Maurya and Gupta empires, many regional kingdoms, the Mughal period and British rule. India became independent on 15 August 1947.

The Solar System

The Solar System consists of the Sun and the objects held by its gravity, including eight planets, their moons, dwarf planets, asteroids and comets. Earth is the third planet from the Sun.

Earth and Its Continents

Earth has seven commonly recognized continents and five commonly recognized oceans. Its landscapes include mountains, deserts, forests, plains and rivers, supporting a great variety of life.

Biographies: Lives That Made History

A biography tells the story of a person's life, achievements and influence. Biographies help us understand the ideas, decisions and experiences of people from different times and places.

Football

Football, also called soccer in some countries, is played by two teams of eleven players. The aim is to score by getting the ball into the opposing team's goal. The FIFA World Cup is the sport's leading international tournament.

Computers and Technology

Computers process information and help people communicate, learn, design and solve problems. Modern technology includes networks, robotics, artificial intelligence and digital tools.

Literature

Literature includes stories, novels, poetry, plays and other written works. It preserves ideas, explores human experiences and shares cultures across generations.
